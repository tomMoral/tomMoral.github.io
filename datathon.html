BegRohu Datathon 2026

  Dates: April 7–10, 2026

  Location: BegRohu, France

About the event

The BegRohu Datathon is an intensive four-day event bringing together researchers and engineers to explore openly available neuroscience datasets. The core idea is to take publicly released recordings across several brain imaging and electrophysiology modalities and benchmark state-of-the-art data analysis approaches on them.

Participants will form small teams, choose one or more datasets, and apply or adapt their methods — whether classical signal processing, machine learning, or deep learning — in a collaborative and reproducible way.

Modalities

The datathon will cover open datasets from the following neuroscience modalities:

fMRI EEG MEG iEEG

Goals

  • Identify and document reusable open datasets across modalities.
  • Test and compare analysis pipelines in a shared environment.
  • Foster cross-disciplinary collaborations between methods and domain experts.
  • Produce reproducible, open-source results that benefit the community.

Practical information

This event is on invitation only, but we encourage interested researchers to reach out if they would like to participate or contribute datasets.
